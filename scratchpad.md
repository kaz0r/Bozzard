## Architecture / guardrails

- glTF budgets: 1M vertices, 3M indices, 4096 surfaces, 512 MiB unique decoded images, 128 MiB decoded buffers, 32 MiB per source, 128 MiB source dependencies. Unsupported skins/animations/morphs/required extensions rejected.
- Renderer stages model geometry/textures across frames and shares vertex buffers/images within a model. ModelUploadStats reports memory, surfaces/images, preparation and observed upload slices (not GPU time). Opaque then center-sorted transparent passes; authored sun/ambient/shadows, no culling/IBL yet.
